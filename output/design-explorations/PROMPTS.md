# Autoroo — generation prompts

Mode: built-in image-generation tool. Three separate generation calls, one per concept. These are visual mockups for selection before implementation.

## A — Googly Getaway

```text
Use case: ui-mockup, logo-brand.
Create one polished high-fidelity 16:9 landscape desktop GAME TITLE SCREEN concept for the silly endless 3D driving game AUTOROO. This is a full-bleed game menu mockup with an original designed wordmark, not a browser, not a SaaS landing page, no device frame. Art direction A: GOOGLY GETAWAY. Be boldly funny, beautifully composed, readable and professionally art-directed.
Actual premise: a cobalt-blue low-poly sports car (#0b3d82) jumps over traffic and barrel-rolls sideways between lanes on a New York-like blue-hour city street. Traffic includes purple single-deck buses, mustard yellow sedans and teal SUVs. No animals, no drivers, no characters, no race circuits, no coins.
LAYOUT: left 48% of screen is a calm deep ink navy field subtly blending into the full-screen city. Right 52% shows a large delightfully absurd cobalt sports car frozen halfway through a SIDEWAYS BARREL ROLL above a purple single-deck bus, tires pointing diagonally toward the viewer. Dynamic curved cream motion swoosh and a few small hand-drawn stars emphasize the flip. Warm lit low-poly brownstones, trees and streetlamps, blue-hour sky; scene readable but quiet behind UI.
LOGO in upper-left, huge and taking about 43% of screen width: exactly 'AUTOROO', spelled A U T O R O O, seven letters, all in one line. Custom chunky rubber-hose cartoon hand-lettering with irregular bouncing baseline, warm cream AUTO and coral ROO, thick deep navy outline and cream die-cut outer edge, slightly offset navy shadow. The final two O letters contain mismatched small pupil dots so those two O's behave like goofy eyes while still reading clearly as two letter O's. A small playful wiggle underline below. Unique legible silhouette, no standard italic racing font.
Under logo, small two-line cream uppercase tagline: 'ENDLESS FUN.' then 'QUESTIONABLE DRIVING.'
Below tagline a very large punchy butter-yellow pill-rectangle button with thick ink stroke, chunky tactile offset shadow and play triangle. Text exactly 'LET’S DRIVE'. Under it two restrained small text controls: 'Sound on' with a speaker icon, and 'Credits'.
Upper-right small cream diecut ticket: 'PERSONAL BEST' and '000000'. This is the only score item.
Bottom a slim translucent dark control legend in four well-spaced groups with clear cream keyboard keycaps: '← →  Flip lanes' ; '↑ ↓  Speed' ; 'SPACE  Jump' ; 'ESC  Pause'. Use the displayed arrow glyphs correctly.
Typography outside logo: sturdy friendly rounded sans, exceptionally readable. Strong hierarchy, spacious placement, energetic premium indie arcade game. No white boxed dashboard/card enclosing the main menu. No speedometer, current score, social buttons, account, level select or other game modes. No additional text or watermarks. Render as one finished title screen, no descriptive labels outside it.
```

## B — Roadside Nonsense

```text
Use case: ui-mockup, logo-brand.
Create one polished high-fidelity 16:9 landscape desktop GAME TITLE SCREEN concept for the silly endless 3D driving game AUTOROO. This is a full-bleed game menu mockup with an original designed wordmark, not a browser, not a SaaS page, no device frame. Art direction B: ROADSIDE NONSENSE, a witty misprinted traffic-sign identity with charming flat graphic design and stylized low-poly city illustration. Funny and clean, not grunge, not childish clipart.
Actual premise: a cobalt-blue sports car (#0b3d82) jumps over traffic and barrel-rolls sideways between lanes on a New York-like urban street at blue hour. Traffic: purple SINGLE-DECK bus, yellow sedan, teal SUV. No animals, no drivers or human characters, no coins, no race circuits.
COMPOSITION distinctly asymmetrical: the left third is a tall butter-yellow roadside sign panel with rounded irregular corners, tilted counterclockwise only 2 degrees. It floats above a dusk asphalt city backdrop. At top of the sign, an original compact logo exactly 'AUTOROO', spelled A U T O R O O, all seven letters in one line. Wordmark is bold near-black custom cut-paper rounded block lettering, the two ending O's are two wobbly tire letters with simple off-center hubs, and a bent upward road-arrow curves from below the word back over its roof to suggest an absurd hop. Slightly jumbled bouncing baseline, charming unexpected proportions; crystal-clear name. Small cobalt and cream cut-paper accent behind the wordmark. The entire yellow sign is the visual brand device, not a diamond that crowds the letters.
Beneath logo, ample air then the small black tagline 'ENDLESS FUN.' and 'QUESTIONABLE DRIVING.' Centered on sign. Below a big cobalt-blue button with cream lettering and black offset shadow: 'LET’S DRIVE', with simple play triangle. Below two small link-sized controls 'Sound on' and 'Credits', speaker icon. At bottom of this sign three crisp tiny cream keycap groups across two rows: '← →  Flip lanes', '↑ ↓  Speed', 'SPACE  Jump'. Add a small 'ESC  Pause' alongside.
Right two-thirds is a spacious wonderful graphic low-poly urban stunt composition, dramatic three-quarter birds-eye street view. One large cobalt sports car is airborne with its underside partly visible, performing a sideways flip above a purple single-deck city bus. Hand-drawn curved arrow showing the sideways roll. An exaggerated looping cream tire skid mark on the asphalt. Buildings form staggered deep teal and dusty blue geometric silhouettes, warm yellow windows, small trees, chunky street lamps. Muted blue-hour palette makes the butter-yellow sign and cobalt car pop.
Upper-right a slightly tilted small cream inspection-sticker style label, compact and readable: 'PERSONAL BEST' with '000000'.
Flat spot-color silk-screen shapes, subtle paper tactility, thick friendly ink outlines for interface, editorial arcade poster appeal but everything is a plausible game menu. Crisp sharp controls, negative space, no gradients on type, no thin futuristic font, no sterile website cards. No speedometer, current score, accounts, shop, levels, invented modes, or extra interface. Only specified text, no artist annotations, no watermarks. One finished game title screen.
```

## C — Toybox Trouble

```text
Use case: ui-mockup, logo-brand.
Create one polished high-fidelity 16:9 landscape desktop GAME TITLE SCREEN concept for the silly endless 3D driving game AUTOROO. Full-bleed playable game-menu mockup with original logo and carefully designed UI, no browser chrome, no device frame. Art direction C: TOYBOX TROUBLE. A premium playful tactile 3D miniature title screen with inflated marshmallow typography and deadpan silly driving. Beautiful and intentional, not generic mobile advertising.
Actual game premise: one dark cobalt-blue (#0b3d82) angular low-poly sports car jumps over traffic and performs lateral barrel rolls to change lanes on a New York-like urban street. Other vehicles mustard-yellow sedans, teal SUVs and purple single-deck buses. Do not introduce mascots, people, kangaroos, coins, race circuits or other gameplay.
COMPOSITION centered and symmetrical UI, different from a left-side menu. Across the upper middle enormous original 3D logo exactly 'AUTOROO', spelled A U T O R O O, seven letters in ONE LINE, occupying about 62% screen width. Custom plump, softly extruded clay-like or inflatable letters, irregular comical bouncing heights and slightly different rotations. AUTO butter-cream, ROO peachy coral, deep purple soft contact shadows. The first final O is a little squashed and the last O is higher and tilted, suggesting a bounce. Their counters stay open and recognizable. Logo underlined by a little curved blue rubber skid-mark flourish. No generic typed word, no neon tubes. Looks collectible, fun and goofy.
Below logo, centered short crisp warm cream tagline on one or two lines: 'ENDLESS FUN. QUESTIONABLE DRIVING.'
The scene spans the screen: a miniature low-poly blue-hour city on a gently curving road, violet-blue dusk sky, simplified midnight-blue brownstone buildings and trees around the sides, warmly glowing windows and lamps. In the mid-right a prominent cobalt sports car is halfway through a sideways barrel roll over a purple single-deck bus, with puffy stylized tire-smoke arcs and tiny warm cream impact stars. Silly motion and warm tactile clay shading. Leave center-lower foreground clear for menu.
Main primary button lower-middle around 70% screen height, wide soft butter-yellow lozenge with a deep purple tactile 3D shadow, dark ink text exactly 'LET’S DRIVE', play triangle. Below it small restrained controls: speaker icon with 'Sound on', then 'Credits'.
Upper-left small cream arcade-ticket badge with purple ink reading 'PERSONAL BEST' and '000000'. Upper-right clean tiny speaker icon optionally but do not duplicate Sound on text.
At bottom one elegant full-width row of clear keycaps on quiet dark-purple ground: '← →  Flip lanes' ; '↑ ↓  Speed' ; 'SPACE  Jump' ; 'ESC  Pause'. Text outside logo is crisp sturdy rounded sans, highly readable. Enough clear space between logo, artwork, button and controls. No white card enclosing menu, no speedometer, current score, shop, garage, multiplayer, levels, accounts, currency, or additional modes. No additional text, slogans, watermarks or labels. One finished richly designed game title screen.
```
